## Design decisions to confirm

- **Split into one or two scripts?** Sections H–H4d are pre-restart config; H4e–H4g are post-restart. Natural split point at H4f. Two smaller scripts (~35 lines each) vs one ~70-line script?
- **Gunicorn poll in bash vs Python?** The poll loop is 10 lines of bash. Keep it in the bash script, or move to a Python helper?
- **Timeout for install_specific.py subcommands?** before-install does file patches (fast); after-restart does API calls (slower, needs gunicorn). Separate timeouts?
